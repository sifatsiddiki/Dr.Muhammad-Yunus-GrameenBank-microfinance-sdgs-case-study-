# Case Study: Dr. Muhammad Yunus, Grameen Bank & Microfinance

This repository contains an academic group presentation analyzing the revolutionary impact of **Dr. Muhammad Yunus** and the **Grameen Bank** on global microfinance, financial inclusion, and sustainable development. 

This project was developed as part of the **English and Skills for Advanced Study** course.

## Project Overview
The presentation explores how microfinance shifted traditional banking paradigms by treating the impoverished as creditworthy individuals. It evaluates the alignment of Grameen Bank’s objectives with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), while critically addressing the challenges, controversies, and future of microfinance models.

## Key Themes Covered
* **Introduction to Leadership:** Defining what makes Dr. Muhammad Yunus an influential global figure.
* **The Genesis of Grameen Bank:** Historical background and main socio-economic objectives.
* **SDG Alignment:** Impact on SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 5 (Gender Equality), SDG 8 (Decent Work), and more.
* **Global Recognition:** Accolades including the 2006 Nobel Peace Prize, Presidential Medal of Freedom, and Congressional Gold Medal.
* **Criticisms & Challenges:** Analysis of high-interest rates, debt cycles, and the balance between profit and social purpose.
